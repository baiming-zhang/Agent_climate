Methods

Particle position

Prescribed three-dimensional WRF wind and native parameterized turbulent velocity; passive gas does not change the wind.

Hourly concentration

Saved values are hourly means on the native output cells; fine integral-plume residence and regional particles are distinct components.

Plume residence

Finite-travel plume residence is reconstructed from actual saved trajectories, radii and masses; rendering does not add source mass.

Reporting-period conservation

Initial and final inventories include the regional, active-plume and pending-transfer reservoirs. Residuals are retained.

Deposition and units

The actual reporting start is lead10. Never sum cumulative snapshots. No ppb value is inferred without a thermodynamic conversion.

Implementation and scope

Native WRF weather drives the native FLEXPART-WRF derivative and finite-travel integral-plume coupling. Stochastic turbulent dispersion and plume entrainment are parameterized; buildings are display context, not resolved CFD/LES. The private I/O cache changes weather-file retention only; scientific fields, source inputs and mass checks are retained. Raw negative weather species and model limitations remain in the frozen records. These figures do not establish observational forecast accuracy or grid/particle convergence.
