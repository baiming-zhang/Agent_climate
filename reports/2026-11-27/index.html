Scores, missing coverage and what this benchmark does not prove
AgentPaired questionsMean relative L2 · controlWith prompts
Seven agents and two conditions were launched. Six have ten jointly scorable primary questions; GPT-6-Luna has no comparable primary pair and is not assigned a zero error. This was one run per condition; small differences near floating-point precision are not evidence of a robust improvement. The arms compare the prompt bundle with a control, not separate ablations of every individual skill.
The ten-question analytical reference and core scorer were independently checked over 49,998 samples. The audit also retained a model-label-dependent batch-export issue. Passing these field checks does not prove that an agent performed first-principles time marching.
For the next protocol: require actual dynamics, preserve every computed revision, and use short physical tests before committing to long production.
