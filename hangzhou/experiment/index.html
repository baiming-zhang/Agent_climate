What changed after the first request?

The initial request was: “Simulate SO₂ emissions and their spatial distribution in Hangzhou over the next 15 days.” A separate fresh task received only that sentence. After the first result was frozen, the controller supplied fifteen corrections one at a time, freezing each actual result before the next. The controller had seen the correction list; it did not claim to erase its own context. Initial-task isolation does not erase generic model knowledge.

Completed experiment does not mean validated air quality.

All sixteen original scientific versions are preserved. The terrain correction includes a genuine continuous CPU particle calculation, but its low particle count and failed new GPU agreement are explicit limitations. Separate delivery engineering has completed a continuous 353,894,400-particle CPU run, 1,024 times the original terrain-case count, in 7,364.23 seconds (2 h 02 min 44 s). All 360 native hourly fields are saved. The maximum mass residual is 6.23×10⁻¹⁰ kg against the unchanged 10⁻⁷ kg tolerance; 7,249 collision-limit truncations are disclosed. This is not another scientific prompt and does not overwrite earlier failures. Open the completed model or its native Scientific analysis.

Substantive changes

Source evidence. Early hypothetical sector sources were replaced with four published EIA design outlets, with outlet coordinates, emission rates, dimensions and temperatures. The conditional total is 4.519 kg/h, or 1,626.84 kg over 360 hours. Actual future operation, the gas-flow reference basis and geodetic datum have not been independently verified. Four documented outlets are not a complete Hangzhou emissions inventory.

Atmospheric transport. The early two-dimensional relative field evolved into a passive three-dimensional stochastic particle model with height-dependent forecast wind, parameterized turbulent diffusion, a bulk stability diagnostic and simplified buoyant rise. The real GFS forecast column is repeated horizontally. There is no resolved terrain-induced wind, ambient vertical flow or building downwash. This is a custom model, not WRF, FLEXPART, CFD or LES.

Continuity and accounting. Later versions explicitly verify state propagation through all 360 hours without daily reset. Airborne mass, deposition, chemical removal and export are tracked separately. The terrain model adds reversible and irreversible surface stores; re-emission recycles internal mass rather than adding an external source. A closed ledger establishes numerical accounting, not concentration accuracy.

Background and sampling. An inadequately validated surface-only background candidate was excluded. Results therefore show source increments with zero initial and incoming background, not total real ambient SO₂. “Ground” concentration is a defined 0–25 m AGL volume mean. It is not a 2 m measurement. Units, coordinates, source release heights and particle occupancy were checked explicitly.

Numerical refinement. Horizontal density bins were refined to 500 m, particle counts studied up to 353.89 million per realization, vertical bins increased to 52 layers and timestep reduced to 30 s. These are distinct studies with their own counts and controls. Several local and full-field convergence criteria remain unmet. Particle-count and bin refinement do not create finer meteorological information.

Terrain and surface exchange. Actual GLO90 DSM data provide bilinear terrain collision and terrain-normal reflection. Surface contact uptake, retention, fixation and conditional return are represented with uncalibrated coefficients. The forecast return gate stayed closed for all 360 hours. A warm/dry surface-only override demonstrated the mechanism; it is not a claim that real forecast weather produced re-emission.

Evidence that was not promoted to a success

Early flat-ground CPU/GPU agreement does not qualify the changed terrain model. Its strict long-run GPU comparison failed. A separate bounded localization found tiny initial floating-point differences amplified along state-dependent trajectories, without demonstrating a semantic implementation bug. No tolerance was relaxed and no “repaired GPU” was promoted. The original 345,600-particle terrain case also cannot inherit high-count convergence evidence from another physical scheme.

The requested 2 mm rainfall allowance is not an independent forecast-error result here: the preserved rainfall comparison uses the same supplied forecast on both backends and therefore gives zero implementation difference. It does not validate rainfall against observations or an independent weather solver.

Presentation and timing

Vancouver-style three-dimensional geographic rendering and separate Scientific analysis are reused on an independent presentation track. No Vancouver numerical field or city model becomes Hangzhou input. Early two-dimensional results remain two-dimensional. Preserved original versions use daily means. The completed high-count case additionally saves genuine hourly native fields: queries read all 360 hourly, 500 m, 52-layer float64 arrays. Its regional graphics use conservative 2 km averages every three hours, with scalar interpolation; display resolution is not native numerical resolution. Grey volume opacity encodes concentration illustratively: SO₂ gas itself is invisible.

Each version and stepwise comparison is planned as a 15-second presentation, after reference-frame review. Every frame is rendered from interpolated data or geometry, with no image crossfade, temporal RGB blending or overlaid neighboring frames. Near-outlet graphics integrate residence along actual accepted paths. This systematic diagnostic selects one member per source/release event through hour 336 and eight members during the final 24 hours, verified against the original subset and saved final states. Display expansion factors are separate from actual sampled mass; this is not the full-count near-outlet concentration or an unbiased random sample. Thirty-second path segmentation can remain visible. The low-to-high-count full-domain daily-field relative L1 difference averages 139.8%; this comparison does not establish local convergence.

Time is reported as observable Agent activity, data/model work, dedicated rendering, and other or inseparable work. Agent activity is a non-tool wall-clock proxy, not an internal inference timer. Overlapping intervals are unioned, and worker versus controller activity is distinguished. Read the current measured timing snapshot.
