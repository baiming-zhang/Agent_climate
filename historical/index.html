ABOUT THIS SCENE

A geographic city. A modelled plume.

Explore conditional releases from three industrial sources, or switch to the71.5-hour December2021 regional result. Display colour and opacity do not alter concentration.

Near-source calculations use Briggs rise and Gaussian puffs; regional calculations use WRF+FLEXPART with16 output layers. Buildings are simplified; display heights are exaggerated1.8×. Building wakes and full CFD are not resolved.

Values are modelled source contributions, not measured air quality. The original success gate failed. The new15-day forecast is separate from this historical case.
